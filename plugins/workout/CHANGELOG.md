# Changelog: Workout tracker

## 0.5.0

- The app's logo is shown next to its name in the toolbar and as the browser tab icon.
- New to the app? You can try it free for 24 hours before subscribing.

## 0.4.0

- A user manual, opened from **Manual** (the ? button) in the toolbar. It explains the setup,
  a guided workout, the voice coach, the calendar, progress photos and the monthly email step by
  step, and can be read before you sign up.

## 0.3.0

- A voice coach: during a workout your phone counts down before each exercise and at the end of
  every rest, announces sets, rests and the next exercise, and cheers you on. Choose a female or
  male voice and a calm, normal or motivational style, or mute it, from the toolbar.
- Encouraging words during rests and after every workout, with what went better than last time
  and your personal records.
- A calendar of your workouts: see a day, a month or a year, with your time, calories, sets,
  repetitions and streaks. Tap a day for its details.
- Progress photos: add a starting photo when you set up (or later in your profile), a photo for
  every month and year, and compare before and after. Only you can see them.
- A monthly summary email on the 1st with your numbers and a link to your calendar. You can turn
  it off in your profile.
- Suggested repetitions now depend on how hard each exercise is: fewer for pull-ups, more for
  calf raises.
- You stay signed in while a workout is running.

## 0.2.0

- The workout tracker now makes routines for you. Tell it your year of birth, height and weight
  (in cm or ft, kg or lb), your experience and your goal, and where you train: at the gym, at
  home or outside. For home, tick the equipment you have.
- You get three routines for each place, each starting with a warm-up, with an estimate of the
  time and the calories. You can have them made again from your profile.
- A guided workout made for your phone: a moving figure shows each exercise, the app suggests
  how many repetitions to do, you enter how many you did, and it moves on to the next exercise
  by itself, with rest timers in between.
- What you enter is saved by itself after 10 seconds. Without a connection it waits on your
  phone and is sent when you are back online.
- After the workout you see the total time, the time for each exercise, the calories (an
  estimate) and what the app suggests for next time.

## 0.1.1

- The app now has its own storage, ready for saving your workouts. Nothing changes for you
  yet.

## 0.1.0

- The first preview of the workout tracker. You open it with your DevQuake account, without
  signing in again.
- A sample week shows what the app will look like. Logging your own workouts comes next.
