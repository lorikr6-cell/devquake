# Changelog — My vault

## 0.1.0

- Keep private information in vault entries: a note, secret fields (like PINs) and files, encrypted on your device before they are saved.
- Each entry opens only with its vault key (shown once to you) and the answers to three questions you choose: a date, text, a number, one or several choices.
- Keep entries private forever, or choose people from your DevQuake referrals who receive the key if you do not use DevQuake for a number of days or months (optionally not before a date). You are warned by email before.
- Every try to open an entry is recorded; after 3 wrong tries it is locked for 36 hours, you get an email and can review who tried and what they answered, and unlock it.
