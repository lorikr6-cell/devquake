# Changelog — Utility bill manager

## 0.1.0

- Add your utilities (electricity, gas, water, heating, phone, cable TV, internet, online hosting, Xbox, Microsoft, Apple, Steam or anything else) and collect their bills month by month.
- Upload the provider’s PDF: the app tries to read the total, consumption, unit price and due date; anything it cannot read you type into the same form.
- Share a utility with an invite link, a QR code or directly with people from your DevQuake referrals. Only the people on a utility can see its bills.
- “Individual meter required”: everyone sends a photo of their meter with the index for every bill; the app tries to read the index from the photo.
- Fair split: consumption × unit price for each person, with anything the meters do not cover shared equally; without meters the bill is split equally.
- The manager records what each person paid (cash, card or other); paying more or less is carried over to the next bill. Paid bills get a green check mark, others a warning.
- Comments on every bill, a month and year calendar of your own part, and statistics on consumption, payments and prices per category.
- Your name and address in your profile, shown only to the people you share a utility with.
